# Design System: Portafolio FISC

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "Academic Dossier"**

Portafolio FISC focuses on institutional elegance and reliability. The visual language uses a "paper-based" metaphor, simulating high-quality academic stationery and official document packets. It aims to provide a calm, premium experience for students who need to organize their semester work with official precision.

## 2. Colors

A warm, tactile palette that avoids the coldness of generic SaaS tools.

### Primary (FISC Green)
- **FISC Emerald** (#123D25): The institutional heart. Used for primary actions, branding, and structural anchors.
- **Deep Forest** (#1C5234): Hover states and interactive depth.

### Surface (Warm Neutrals)
- **Antique Paper** (#F5F0E8): Main page background.
- **Pressed Linen** (#EDE5D4): Sidebar and secondary section backgrounds.
- **Virgin Vellum** (#FFFDF7): Cards, document sheets, and focus areas.

### Ink & Rule
- **Charcoal Ink** (#1C1A17): Primary text for maximum legibility.
- **Dusty Sepia** (#5C5549): Muted text and metadata.
- **Stone Rule** (#DED5C4): Borders and structural separators.

## 3. Typography

**Display Font:** Lora (Serif) for headings to convey academic prestige and tradition.
**UI/Body Font:** Inter (Sans-serif) for clean, functional readability in forms and data-heavy areas.

## 4. Visual Metaphor: The Dossier

The core of the identity is the physical dossier.
- **Stacked Sheets**: Multiple layers with subtle rotations and offsets.
- **Side Tabs**: Mono-spaced labels for section organization.
- **Tactile Texture**: Subtle fractal noise to simulate paper grain.
- **Restrained Shadows**: Soft, layered depth that feels intentionally placed.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
- Refined rectangular shapes with 6px radius.
- Primary buttons use the institutional green; outline buttons focus on the border hierarchy.

### The Assistant (Wizard)
- 3-column layout (Desktop) separating navigation, action, and live results.
- Sidebar with functional completion checkmarks.
- "Review" summary styled as a clean document checklist.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** maintain a warm, paper-like atmosphere.
- **Do** use institutional green with discipline.
- **Do** emphasize alignment and negative space for a "premium" feel.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use neon, vibrant gradients, or glassmorphism.
- **Don't** use pure black or pure white; stick to the warm neutral range.
- **Don't** allow visual clutter; every object must feel part of the dossier.
